What's in 3.1

  • One product: NovaTV is now TeleNova. Every movie and episode opens its list of Telegram links (tested, exact season/episode), followed by the other sources.
  • POV, its repository and the add-ons that gave no content were removed from the build (47 add-ons instead of 63).
  • Much faster search: one Telegram request per query, a rate limit no longer freezes it, 12-second limit, the list opens as soon as Telegram answered.
  • The build is downloaded in two parts and checked (SHA-256) by the wizard and by the Windows installer.
